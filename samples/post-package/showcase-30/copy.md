# Post package — 30 s capability showcase

Facts used (all from the build, nothing invented):

- **30.2 s** · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · **8.2 MB** · −14.6 LUFS · TP −1.5
- eight chapters: الهوك · العمق · القص والكابشن · بنك الحركة · 3D · الشرح · الأرقام · كل الأوضاع
- **depth beat** at 2.3 s: the person is cut from the background, «أصل» sits above his head, «94» is split behind it
- **7 modes** named on the roster card · **13 styles** · **102 moves** · **12 gates**
- this film shows **4** of the 7 in footage: talking reel, motion 3D, web demo, cover/end card
- **montage** has its own sample (`showcase-modes.mp4` + `montage.mp4`), **podcast** has `showcase-modes.mp4`
- **product review** is the only mode with no footage anywhere in the repo
- 12/12 gates, 0 blockers

## Hooks (ranked)

2. **12 بوابة / صفر ملاحظات** — the fail-closed differentiator. Best for a technical audience.

## LinkedIn (English)

Most AI video tools stop at "it rendered." The hard part is shipping something that survives review.

This 30-second piece has eight chapters: a spoken hook, a real depth beat (the subject cut from the background with a word passing behind his head), word-synced captions, a six-layer motion bank, 3D, a live site captured inside a phone, a data chapter, and the seven-mode roster.

What makes it shippable is the boring part: **12 gates** that fail the file on their own — black frames, captions out of sync with the waveform, mismatched colour tags, loudness under −14 LUFS. This cut passed all 12 with zero blockers.

One thing the video does not say out loud: 3 of the 7 modes (product review, montage, podcast) are named on the roster but have no footage yet. I'd rather list them than fake them.

## X

30 seconds, 8 chapters, one system: hook → depth cutout (word behind the head) → synced captions → motion bank → 3D → live site in a phone → data → all seven modes.

12 gates block the file if a frame is black, a caption drifts, or the audio misses −14 LUFS. This one passed 12/12.
